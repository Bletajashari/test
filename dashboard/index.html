    How the risk score works

    1. +3 stale: no update in Jira for 60 days or more.
    2. +3 / +2 priority: Highest scores 3, High scores 2.
    3. +3 unresolved dependency: an open "is blocked by" or "depends on" link. A ticket only in Blocked status scores +2.
    4. +3 financial: the summary or labels mention invoicing, billing, finance, payments, payroll, timesheets, tax/VAT, credit notes, purchase orders, rate cards, revenue, accruals, remittance or funding.
    5. +1 no owner: High or Highest priority with no assignee.
    6. Needs management action when a high-priority ticket is blocked or stale, when a financial ticket is blocked, stale or high priority, or when the score reaches 7.
